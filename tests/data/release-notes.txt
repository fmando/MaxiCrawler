MaxiCrawler release notes
=========================

Thanks to everyone who filed reports during the pre-alpha period.

The full changelog lives at https://example.test/changelog and the migration
guide is at https://example.test/docs/migration. Please read both before
upgrading.

Known issues are tracked at https://example.test/issues?state=open. If you hit
something that is not listed there, open a ticket (see https://example.test/support).

A mirror of the archive is available over plain HTTP at
http://mirror.example.test/releases/ for networks that block TLS.

The changelog URL is repeated here on purpose: https://example.test/changelog

Not URLs, and deliberately so:
  - version 1.2.3 of the parser
  - contact us at support@example.test
  - the local file ./maxicrawler.toml
  - ftp://legacy.example.test/old-builds is no longer maintained
  - https:// on its own is not a URL either
